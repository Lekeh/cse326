Phishing

Phishing is when a hacker sends a fake email or message to steal your password, OTP or bank details.

How to find a phishing message:

  • It says "urgent" or "your account will be blocked"
  • The sender email looks strange
  • It has spelling mistakes
  • It asks for your OTP or password

Never click unknown links. Open the official website yourself.

Passwords

A weak password can be guessed very fast by hackers.

  • Use at least 8 characters
  • Use capital letters, numbers and symbols
  • Do not use your name or birthday
  • Do not use the same password everywhere

Never share your password with anyone.

Social Engineering

Here the hacker tricks a person instead of a computer.

  • Pretending to be from the bank or IT department
  • Fake lottery or prize calls
  • Leaving an infected pendrive on the ground

Always verify who is asking before giving any information.

Safe Browsing

The internet has many fake websites and viruses.

  • Check for https in the website address
  • Download software only from official sites
  • Do not do banking on public Wi-Fi
  • Keep your antivirus updated

If you get cheated online, call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in
